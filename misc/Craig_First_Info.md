### HOW IT WORKS

#### 1. Upload your records

At the end of each month, we'll send you your secure upload link.

Please upload:

- Your completed Excel bookkeeping spreadsheet
- Sales invoices
- Purchase invoices
- Receipts
- Bank information
- CIS information, where applicable
- Any other relevant documents

#### 2. We check your information

We'll review everything you've provided and let you know if anything is missing or needs clarification.

#### 3. We complete your bookkeeping

We'll enter your information into your accounting software and:

- Record your income and expenses
- Process sales and purchases
- Categorise transactions
- Reconcile your bank
- Review your bookkeeping records

#### 4. VAT & CIS

Where included in your package, we'll prepare your:

- VAT Return
- CIS records/return

We'll contact you if we need clarification before completing the relevant work.

#### 5. Your monthly review

Once your bookkeeping is complete, we'll review the accounts and prepare your agreed monthly reports.

These may include:

- Profit & Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Cash position
- VAT position
- CIS summary

#### 6. Bookkeeping complete

We'll notify you when your monthly bookkeeping is complete and make your reports available through your client portal.

#### 7. Next month

At the start of the next cycle, we'll send you your upload reminder and the process starts again.
