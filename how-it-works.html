How your monthly bookkeeping works

Simple for you.
We handle the rest.

Securely upload your records; we do everything after that. Here is the exact process, start to finish, every single month.

Six moves.
Same order.
Every month.

Scroll through the process. This is not a sales diagram; it is the actual operating rhythm your books run on.

Start this process for your firm→
01 / 06

Secure client portal

We give you a private portal, your dedicated folder inside it, and the app on your phone. Everything lives in one place from day one.

02 / 06

You upload

Snap receipts as you get them, and drop your spreadsheet, invoices and statements into your folder each month. Ten seconds a document, done from the van.

03 / 06

We review

We check what has come in and message you if anything is missing or unclear. One message, not twenty questions.

04 / 06

We complete your bookkeeping

Your records are processed and your accounting software updated by a construction bookkeeper, not just an algorithm.

05 / 06

We reconcile and check

Accounts reconciled against the bank, VAT and CIS dealt with where your package includes them, reverse charge coded both directions.

06 / 06

Monthly reports land

Your agreed reports arrive in the portal with a plain-English note on what changed and why, and a message that your month is finished.

Set your watch
by it.

Records in by the 10th. CIS filed by the 19th. Reports by month end. The same rhythm every month, with reminders in the portal so nothing relies on memory. Records after the 10th can still be done, but carry a £25 late surcharge, which exists to keep the deadline real. In practice the app means almost nobody ever pays it.
