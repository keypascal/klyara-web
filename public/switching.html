Switching to Klyara

Leaving your bookkeeper
is a ten-day job. Ours.

People stay with bad bookkeeping for years because switching feels like moving house. It is not: professional handover is a standard, polite, ten-working-day process, and every part of it is our job, not yours.

The ten days,
day by day.

Days 1–2

You say yes; we say hello

You sign our engagement letter and tell your current bookkeeper you are moving: one short email, and we give you the exact wording. No confrontation, no explanations owed. Professional courtesy handles the rest.

Days 2–5

The professional clearance dance

We write to the outgoing firm for professional clearance and your records: it is a standard letter they receive weekly. They are professionally obliged to hand over your books, backups and schedules. You do nothing.

Days 3–7

HMRC authorisations move

Agent authorisations for Self Assessment, CIS, VAT and PAYE re-point to us: submitted online, usually approved within days. Your filings continue uninterrupted; deadlines do not care about handovers, so neither do we.

Days 5–10

Rebuild, reconcile, go live

Books land in your new stack, opening balances reconciled to the penny, the app goes on your phone, and the first monthly cycle starts. Anything odd we find in the old books is reported plainly, not dramatically.
