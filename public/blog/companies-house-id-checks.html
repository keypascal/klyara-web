Director? Companies House now wants to see your ID

30 Jun 2026 · 9 min read · by the Klyara team

30 second versionBreak it down for me

  • Every director and PSC must verify their identity with Companies House.
  • New directors already must; existing ones are swept in at confirmation-statement time.
  • It is free and takes about ten minutes with photo ID via GOV.UK One Login.
  • Unverified means blocked filings, and acting unverified is an offence.

A letter or email about "identity verification" from Companies House is not a scam, for once. Under the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act, every company director and person with significant control must verify their identity, and the deadlines are rolling through 2026.

The timetable

New directors have needed verification since November 2025, before appointment. Existing directors verify by their company's next confirmation statement date, which is why deadlines are landing on different firms in different months all year. Ignore it and you cannot file, and an unverified director is committing an offence, which is a strong word for something that takes ten minutes.

How to do it

Free, online, via GOV.UK One Login with photo ID, or through an authorised agent such as an accountant if the online route does not suit. Do it once; it covers all your directorships.

Why this exists

Companies House spent decades as a filing cabinet that believed anything. Verification is part of turning it into a register that means something, alongside powers to query and clean up nonsense filings. For legitimate firms it is mild admin; for the phoenix-company merchants who undercut you with no intention of paying anyone, it makes life properly harder. On balance, the trade wins.

Our Ltd clients get nudged before their date as part of the monthly rhythm, because a blocked filing is a stupid way to have a bad week. The full 2026 rule radar is here.

Who needs verifying, and how

  1. 1
    List everyone in scope

    Every director and every person with significant control. In family companies this routinely includes a spouse holding shares.

  2. 2
    Choose your route

    Directly through GOV.UK One Login with photo ID and a face scan, or through an authorised agent.

  3. 3
    Complete it

    Ten to fifteen minutes. Passport or driving licence, and a phone with a camera.

  4. 4
    Save your personal code

    Verification produces an identifier that links you to your Companies House filings.

  5. 5
    Provide it where required

    Filings increasingly require verified identities to be linked before they are accepted.

If you areYour deadline
A new directorBefore appointment: verification is required up front
An existing directorBy your company's next confirmation statement
A PSC who is not a directorIn line with the rolling implementation
Filing on behalf of othersAuthorised agents must be registered themselves

Deadlines run by confirmation-statement date, so two similar companies can have very different deadlines.

The practical risk

An unverified director means rejected filings, and rejected filings mean a company drifting toward being overdue on the public register. Late filing carries its own penalties, and continuing to act while unverified is an offence in its own right.

It is free, quick, and permanent. The only real cost is discovering the requirement on the day a filing bounces.
