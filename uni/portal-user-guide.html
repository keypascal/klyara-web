Guide 03 · For clients

Using your Klyara portal.

Everything you need it for, in the order you will need it. Five minutes to read, and you will not need to read it twice.

What it is. One place for the paperwork side of your business. You photograph receipts, we file them into your books, and anything we produce for you — your figures, your VAT return, your CIS statements — lands here for you to keep.

What it is not. It is not your accounting software and you do not have to learn it. There is nothing to fill in, nothing to reconcile and nothing that breaks if you ignore it for a week.

1 · Getting in the first time

  1. Go to app.klyara.com

    On your phone. That is where you will use it ninety-nine times out of a hundred.

  2. Create your account

    Your name, your business name, your mobile, your email, and a password of at least eight characters. Use the email you actually read.

  3. Open the email we send you

    Tap the link in it on the same phone. That confirms it is really you.

  4. Answer three questions

    Which plan, where your books are kept (QuickBooks, Xero or FreeAgent — pick QuickBooks if you are on none of them and we will sort it), and whether VAT and CIS apply to you. Thirty seconds. You can change any of it later by ringing us.

2 · Put it on your home screen

Do this once and the portal behaves like any other app on your phone: its own icon, full screen, and the camera is one tap away instead of four.

PhoneWhat to do
iPhoneOpen app.klyara.com in Safari. Tap the Share button at the bottom — the square with an arrow coming out of it. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
AndroidA bar appears offering to install it. Tap it. If you missed it, open the Account page and there is an Install Klyara button.

iPhone only — worth knowing

On an iPhone we cannot send you a notification until the app is on your home screen. That is Apple's rule, not ours. If you want to know the moment a receipt is filed or a document is ready, do the three taps above.

3 · Sending a receipt

This is the whole point of the thing, and it is one tap.

  1. Tap Receipts, then Photograph a receipt

    The camera opens. Take the picture and tap use.

  2. That is it

    The photo shrinks itself before it sends, so it works on one bar of signal and barely touches your data. You will see it appear in the list with the label Received.

  3. Several at once

    Tap Choose from files instead and select as many as you like. Good for the glovebox clear-out on a Sunday night.

Getting a photo we can actually use. Flat, all four corners in the picture, and enough light to read the total. Crumpled is fine — we deal with crumpled all day. Faded, folded across the total, or half in shadow is not.

Photograph it when you are handed it, not when you remember. Two seconds at the counter beats twenty minutes in the van in March.

4 · Your documents

Your monthly figures, VAT returns, CIS statements and any letters we write for you. Tap one and it opens; from there you can save it or send it on. Everything here is yours to keep — if you ever leave us, you leave with all of it.

We will tell you when something new appears, so you do not need to keep checking.

5 · Asking about anything else

Things we can take off your hands: someone answering your phone, a website that actually shows up in local search, payroll, an honest look at what you are paying for insurance. Tap Ask about this and we will come back with a straight answer and a price. Nobody will ring you up trying to sell you something.

6 · Your details

The Account page has your name, mobile, your plan and how to reach us. Change your name or number there any time.

Changing your email address

Ring us — we do it with you on the phone. It is deliberately not a form. If it were, anyone who got hold of your unlocked phone could type in their own address and take the account.

One thing we will never do

We will never ring, text or email asking for your password, your bank details or a code from your phone. If someone does, it is not us. Hang up and ring us on the number above.
