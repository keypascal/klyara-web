# BLOCK 2 — The compliance chain (20 min) 🔴

**Deadline: Monday 31 August. This block gates revenue.** Until AML supervision is in place Klyara
cannot lawfully carry on bookkeeping by way of business — not for a fee, not for a deposit. Nothing
else in the plan matters until it is resolved.

### 8. ICB or AAT — which route?
**My recommendation: ICB.** £94 application, £206 practice licence a year and £167–£200 membership,
with **AML supervision included** and 28-day stated processing, plus a professional body, a public
directory listing, discounted PII and engagement letter templates. Registering with HMRC direct is
£300 up front, £400 per premises annually, up to 45 days, and gets you supervision and nothing else.

**But** it depends on Craig's qualifications and which body he can join. That is the question.
*(Detail and sources in `docs/17 §1`.)*

**What I need:** the route named, by Monday 31 August.

### 9. Confirm the application goes in on Tuesday 1 September.
Every "when can we take clients" decision keys off this date. Applied 1 September plus 28 days means
supervision around **Tuesday 29 September** — the whole plan is built on that. If it slips a week,
everything behind it slips a week, so tell me on the day if it does not go in.

**What I need:** confirmation on Tuesday 1 September that it is submitted, and the reference number.

### 10. What can Klyara legally do *today*, before supervision?
The pack assumes: education **yes**, waitlist **yes**, deposits **no**, bookkeeping by way of business
**no**. I want that confirmed rather than assumed, because around forty pieces of content are written
in two versions and I need to know which one ships on 14 September.

**What I need:** confirmation by Monday 31 August, so the launch content is locked before setup week.

### 11. PII insurance — arranged, and at what cover level?

**What I need:** quotes requested Tuesday 1 September, cover level decided by Friday 11 September.

### 12. Who is the AML-nominated officer, and is the firm-wide risk assessment written?
MLR 2017 requires both. `docs/12` assumes they exist by client one.

**What I need:** the officer named by Monday 31 August; the written risk assessment by

### 25. Do we hold the launch prices, or raise them now?

My recommendation is **hold**, and I would defend it: launching at £59–£329/mo ex VAT with no reviews,
no case studies and no licence displayed yet means price is doing a real job — it is buying the first
ten clients, and those are worth more as proof than as revenue.

But the market data says Solo at £59 is **below the published price of the CIS return on its own**
(£80–£200/month standalone, from £95 at the nearest specialist), and Trade at £199 is a third to two
thirds under the direct comparator (£300–£500 for a contractor-with-subbies bundle).

**What I need:** a yes to hold, or a decision to move now, by Monday 31 August. If we hold, I need
agreement to 26 and 27 as well, because without them the low price becomes permanent by default.

### 26. Can I add "founding client rate, held for twelve months" to every price we publish?

This is the cheapest decision in the whole pack. It costs nothing today and it makes the increase in
September 2027 a scheduled event rather than a confrontation. Without it, every client who joins in
the next twelve months has an implied permanent price. It is the same decision as item 17 — answer
them together.

**What I need:** yes or no by Monday 31 August. If yes, it goes on the pricing page, the proposal
template and the schedule of services during setup week, before launch.

### 27. Do you accept the September 2027 target prices?

Solo £89 · Start £149 · Trade £279 · Pro £449, all per month ex VAT — increases of £30, £50, £80 and
£120. Every one of those still sits under the published comparator, so the position stays *better
value than the specialists* rather than *cheapest*.

**What I need:** agreement in principle by Monday 31 August, so the founding-rate wording has a number
behind it. It can be revised in twelve months with real data. Put the review date in the diary now:
**September 2027**.

### 28. What do we charge for the one-off services we are currently giving away?

The nearest specialist charges £400 for a gross payment status application, £250 for reverse charge
setup and from £400 for a company formation. My proposal is **£195, £125 and £150** respectively, all
ex VAT, with the first two included free on Trade and Pro (reverse charge setup free at onboarding on
any tier).

The gross payment status one matters most. On £120,000 of labour invoiced in a year, the 20% no longer
deducted at source is **£24,000** that stays in the client's bank until the tax is actually due. That
is a defined piece of work with an obvious number attached, and it is an excellent first transaction
with someone who is not ready to move their whole bookkeeping yet.

**What I need:** approval of the rate card in `docs/21 PART 2`, or your changes to it, by Friday 11
September.

### 29. Is 10% of the monthly fee, ongoing, the right partner commission?

On a Trade client at £199/mo ex VAT that is **£19.90 a month, £238.80 a year**, for as long as they
stay. An accountant who sends four builders is about £955 a year for work they did not want.

The alternative is a one-off finder's fee, which is cheaper and produces one referral instead of a
partner who thinks about you in October. Ongoing is the right answer, but it is your margin.

**What I need, by Friday 11 September:** the rate confirmed, and agreement to the term that Klyara
will not offer year-end accounts to an introduced client where the partner provides them. That term is
what makes the programme sellable and it has to be real. `docs/06 §7` has been set to 10% ongoing to
match — it previously said 10–15%.

**DECIDED.** There was a clash: 07:30 Monday was both the cold-call block and the anchor-post slot in

**What I still need, before launch on Monday 14 September:** whose diary it goes in, and confirmation
