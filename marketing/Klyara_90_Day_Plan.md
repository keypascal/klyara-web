# The decision gates

Don't carry on doing something that isn't working because it's in the plan.

| When | Question | If no |
|---|---|---|
| **End of week 2** | Is GBP verified? | Chase it. Nothing local works until it is, and it is the single most common launch failure. |
| **End of month 1** | Four or more discovery calls from Guildford postcodes? | Almost always GBP verification. Check that before changing content. |
| **£150 of ad spend** | Cost per booked call under £40? | Pause ads. Organic, GBP and referrals cost nothing. |
| **End of month 2** | Any accountant partnership conversations live? | Send 20 more. This channel is the fastest route to clients 1–5 and 20 emails is a small sample. |
| **End of month 3** | 8–12 booked calls a month? | Cut the bottom half of the channel map and put those hours into the top half. |
| **End of month 3** | Proposals converting above 25%? | Do not cut prices. Record three calls and listen to whether you're diagnosing or describing. |
| **Any time** | Are you doing more than 2 hours a week on social? | Either it's working and you hire, or you're overproducing for channels that aren't returning. |

# The honest risk list

Once, plainly, then it's your call.

1. **The testimonials.** Until they're off, everything else is building on a liability.
2. **AML timing.** The whole revenue plan sits behind it. If it slips past November the waitlist is
   the only thing keeping the pipeline warm — so keep publishing regardless.
3. **Craig is the whole product.** Every channel here is fronted by one person. That's why it works,
   and it's also the single point of failure. The continuity arrangement isn't paperwork.
4. **Two founders, no signed agreement.** Build Manual §0.3. Not marketing, but it kills more small
   firms than competition does, and marketing that works makes it more urgent, not less.
5. **Capacity arrives faster than hiring.** Craig's ceiling is 20–25 full-service equivalents and the
   hire trigger is 15. Decide the waitlist policy before you need it.
6. **This pack assumes Guildford.** If you end up trading from somewhere else, the local pack and the
   five web pages need the town swapped — the generator makes that a ten-minute job, but somebody has
   to remember to do it.
