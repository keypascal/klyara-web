## What I could not do, and why

**I cannot create accounts on your behalf.** Signing up to Reddit, X, Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat
or the trade forums means entering credentials, accepting terms of service, and clearing bot checks —
all outside what I'm permitted to do, regardless of authorisation. It's also the wrong outcome:
platforms flag auto-registered accounts, and a banned handle on day one is unrecoverable.

**So the pack is built to make you fast instead.** Every profile field is written to the platform's
exact character limit, every image is rendered at the right pixel dimensions, and the setup order is
sequenced so the slow steps start first. Budget **six hours across two sittings** and you'll have the
whole estate live.

**Second thing, flagged once:** your Build Manual is explicit that Klyara can't do bookkeeping by way
of business until AML supervision is in place. Everything here is therefore written
**education-first**, with waitlist CTAs. Direct "hire us" copy exists and is marked `⛔ HOLD` with
the compliant alternative written underneath it. Swap them the day supervision lands.

## Brand system — extracted from the site, not invented

```
--paper  #F4EFE4   warm bone, page ground        --pine   #16352A   deep green, dark sections
--paper2 #ECE5D4   deeper cream                  --pine2  #1D4534   lighter pine, ghost numerals
--card   #FBF8F0   warm white surface            --em     #196A48   emerald, positive/data
--ink    #14231C   green-black, primary text     --brass  #C08A2E   THE accent
--mute   #57685C   muted sage on paper           --warn   #A6592E   terracotta, bad news only
--dim    #87947F   muted sage on pine
```

Type is **Bricolage Grotesque** (display 600–800) / **Instrument Sans** (body 400–700) /
**Spline Sans Mono** (kickers, tags, footers — always uppercase, wide tracking). The actual woff2
files were extracted from your site HTML and converted to static TTF at `brand/fonts/`, so every
asset uses your real fonts rather than a lookalike.

The mark is the angular **K polygon** from your inline SVG — not a typeset letter — either bare in
bone or set in a brass disc. The wordmark is sentence-case `Klyara` on screen and `KLYARA` in print
headers. Headlines end with a full stop.

**One constraint to know:** your embedded fonts are 108-glyph subsets. Em dashes, ticks, crosses and
warning symbols do not exist in them and render as gaps. Every generator routes text through a
`safe()` substitution; if you hand-set copy anywhere else, use `–` and `·` instead.

Full system in `docs/10-brand-guidelines.md`.

One constraint discovered along the way: your embedded fonts are **108-glyph subsets**. Em dashes,
