## `⛔ HOLD` — what the marker means

**This is the one definition. Every other document in the pack uses the marker and none of them
redefine it.**

`⛔ HOLD` on a line, a post, a CTA, a campaign or a directory listing means: **do not publish this
until AML supervision is granted.** It is an internal working-document convention. It never appears
in anything a client or a reader sees, and it is not a note to a designer — it is a compliance gate.

**The condition, in full.** Klyara cannot carry out bookkeeping by way of business until it is
supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes. Supervision comes with the ICB practice licence.

| | |
|---|---|
| Applied | **Tuesday 1 September 2026** — first item of the setup phase |
| Stated processing | **28 days** |
| Expected grant | **around Tuesday 29 September 2026** |
| The switch | **the day the licence number is in hand** — not the expected date, not "late September" |

**Everything carrying the marker switches on that day, together.** There is no partial release and
nothing goes early on the strength of an expected date. Chase the ICB weekly from day 28. If the
licence slips, the marked items slip with it and the education-first content carries on unchanged —
which is why the plan is built so that nothing else depends on it.

**What the marker is attached to.** Direct "hire us" copy always has the compliant waitlist
alternative written underneath it, so switching over is a swap, not a rewrite. Marked items live in
`docs/03` (the conversion posts and three GBP variants), `docs/05` (the HOLD-UNTIL-AML CTAs, each
paired with its POST-AML version), `docs/01` (Bark.com, and the ICB/AAT member directory, which
cannot be listed before the licence exists anyway), `docs/06` (Google Ads, built and paused; the
local press release), and `docs/11` (Guildford Google Ads targeting). `docs/14` and `docs/16` both
carry the same one-line switch-over task on the day it lands, and `docs/20` scores the slip as a
risk.

## Brand system — extracted from the site, not invented

```
--paper  #F4EFE4   warm bone, page ground        --pine   #16352A   deep green, dark sections
--paper2 #ECE5D4   deeper cream                  --pine2  #1D4534   lighter pine, ghost numerals
--card   #FBF8F0   warm white surface            --em     #196A48   emerald, positive/data
--ink    #14231C   green-black, primary text     --brass  #C08A2E   THE accent
--mute   #57685C   muted sage on paper           --warn   #A6592E   terracotta, bad news only
--dim    #87947F   muted sage on pine
```

Type is **Bricolage Grotesque** (display 600–800) / **Instrument Sans** (body 400–700) /
**Spline Sans Mono** (kickers, tags, footers — always uppercase, wide tracking). The actual woff2
files were extracted from your site HTML and converted to static TTF at `brand/fonts/`, so every
asset uses your real fonts rather than a lookalike.

The mark is the angular **K polygon** from your inline SVG — not a typeset letter — either bare in
bone or set in a brass disc. The wordmark is sentence-case `Klyara` on screen and `KLYARA` in print
headers. Headlines end with a full stop.

**One constraint to know:** your embedded fonts are 108-glyph subsets. Em dashes, ticks, crosses and
warning symbols do not exist in them and render as gaps. Every generator routes text through a
`safe()` substitution; if you hand-set copy anywhere else, use `–` and `·` instead.

Full system in `docs/10-brand-guidelines.md`.
