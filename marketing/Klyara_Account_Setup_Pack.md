## Before you start — 5 decisions, made

**1. Handle.** Use this order, take the first available, then use it *everywhere* for consistency:
```
klyara  →  klyarahq  →  klyara_uk  →  getklyara  →  klyaraback (last resort)
```
Whatever you land on, **write it here and use it on every platform**, even where a better one is free.
Consistency beats perfection: `@klyarahq` everywhere is worth more than the perfect handle on three
platforms and a mess on the rest.

**2. Display name.** `Klyara` on personal-style platforms. `Klyara — Construction Back Office` where
the field allows more and search matters (LinkedIn, GBP, YouTube, directories).

**3. Email for signups.** Do **not** use `hello@klyara.com` — that's your public inbox and it will
drown. Create `social@klyara.com` and use it for every platform registration. One inbox, all the
password resets and verification codes in one place.

**4. Password + 2FA.** Every account into Bitwarden (already in the stack), 2FA on every single one.
A hijacked social account for a firm that holds clients' financial data is a
reputational event you cannot talk your way out of.

**5. Who owns the account.** Facebook Pages and LinkedIn Company Pages are owned by a *personal*
profile, not by the business. Set both founders as admins from day one. Businesses lose their pages
when the one admin leaves.
