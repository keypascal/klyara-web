# Klyara — A Year of Google Business Profile Posts
### 52 posts, dated, written. The highest-return five minutes in the whole pack.

---

## Why this document exists

Google Business Profile posts **expire after seven days.** A profile with a fresh post every week
looks active; one with a post from March looks abandoned, and prospects read that as "are they still
trading?"

Posting weekly forever is trivially easy for two weeks and then quietly stops happening. So here is a
year of them, dated, tied to the real deadline calendar, ready to schedule.

**Rules that make GBP posts work:**
- 80–120 words is the sweet spot. Google truncates around 1,500 characters but nobody reads that far.
- **First sentence carries the whole thing** — that's what shows in the preview.
- One CTA button. `Learn more` pre-AML, `Book` once supervised.
- Add a photo to every post. Use the rendered assets in `assets/posts/`.
- Mention **Guildford or a named town** in most of them. It is a local ranking and relevance signal.
- Never keyword-stuff. Google devalues it and it reads badly.

**Schedule:** every Monday, 08:00. It takes five minutes with a scheduler, ten by hand.

## The four recurring anchors

Every year these repeat, and between them they carry roughly half the calendar:
```
The 19th of every month     CIS return and payment
7 Aug / 7 Nov / 7 Feb / 7 May   MTD quarterly updates
31 January                  Final declaration + payment on account
5 April                     Tax year end
```

Set a recurring reminder against each. You will never run out of things to post.
