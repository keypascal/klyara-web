The penalty ladder,
rung by rung.

HMRC's late-submission regime works on points, like a driving licence in reverse. Here is the ladder nobody should ever climb.

Step 1

Miss a deadline, earn a point

Every late quarterly update or declaration adds one penalty point to your record.

Step 2

Hit the threshold, pay a fine

Collect enough points for your filing frequency and a fixed £200 penalty lands, then again for each further miss.

Step 3

Pay late, pay interest

Late payment brings its own charges on top, growing the longer the bill sits. The ratchet only turns one way.
