Why "the back office"?

Watch a small building firm for a week and you will see the pattern: a bookkeeper who does not ring back, a website bloke who vanished after launch, a phone contract nobody understands, and the owner doing quotes at 10pm because the day went on the tools.

None of those suppliers talk to each other. Every one of them starts from zero on what your business actually is.

Klyara's bet is simple: if one specialist team runs your books, the thing that touches everything, it can run the rest better and cheaper than five strangers. The books tell us when you need chasing letters, when the phone is costing you jobs, when a website would pay for itself.

We launched with the core done properly: books, CIS, VAT, MTD, plus the phone answering and websites builders ask for first. The rest of the menu rolls out as each piece proves itself on our own business before it is sold on yours.

Our AI vision:
robots carry, humans judge.

Every hour a builder spends typing is an hour stolen from the tools or the family. Our position is simple: machines should carry every carriable hour, and a construction specialist should judge everything that matters.

So the AI reads receipts, answers phones at midnight, chases invoices without embarrassment and drafts the posts. And a named human signs off every number HMRC ever sees, replies to every review in your voice, and phones you when judgement is needed. We adopt new tools the week they earn it, on our own business first, and we will never sell a robot where a human is honestly better.
