KLYARA PRINT SPECIFICATION
Everything in this folder, what size it is, and how to get it printed.
Written 26 August 2026. Paste any section of this straight into an email to a printer.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW THE FILES ARE NAMED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ...-PRINT.pdf     For a commercial printer. Trim size plus 3 mm bleed on every
                    side, crop marks outside the bleed, and a real TrimBox and
                    BleedBox written into the PDF so their software knows where
                    the cut is. Do not print these on an office printer: the page
                    is bigger than the finished piece.

  ...-OFFICE.pdf    Exact finished size, no bleed, no marks. This is the one to
                    print on your own laser printer or take to a high street copy
                    shop. Print at 100%, never "fit to page".

  ...-artwork.pdf   Envelopes. Exactly the finished envelope size. No bleed and no
                    crop marks, because the envelope is already made up.

  ...-guide.pdf     Envelopes. The same artwork with the window position and the
                    Royal Mail clear zones drawn on in dashed lines. It says
                    GUIDE LAYER - DO NOT PRINT across the bottom. Send this to
                    the printer for reference and the -artwork file to print.

3. WHAT TO TELL THE PRINTER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Colour            Supplied in RGB. Convert with your own profile. The hex
                    values and a reference CMYK breakdown are printed on the
                    brand sheet in this folder.

  Rich black        The dark green ground is #16352A. Do not run it as a flat
                    single-plate black. Ask for a rich build, or it will look
                    grey and blotchy across large areas.

  Bleed             3 mm on every side of every -PRINT file. TrimBox and BleedBox
                    are set in the PDF, so an imposition program will pick the
                    trim up automatically.

  Safety            No text sits closer than 4 mm to the trim on any piece.

  Fonts             Fully embedded as subsets. Nothing needs installing.

  Overprint         None set. No spot colours. No transparency flattening needed.

  Stock, suggested  Business cards      400 gsm uncoated, matt laminate optional
                    Letterhead          120 gsm uncoated
                    Compliment slips    120 gsm uncoated, same stock as the letterhead
                    Service sheets      170 gsm silk or uncoated
                    Leaflet             170 gsm silk
                    Brand sheet         whatever is on the shelf

                    Uncoated suits this palette better than gloss. The warm paper
                    colour is printed ink, not the stock, so a plain white
                    uncoated sheet is the right base.

4. ENVELOPES: THE PRINT AREA
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Envelopes are overprinted after they are made, so there is no bleed and no trim.
Whatever you send is what lands on a finished envelope. Three things constrain
where the artwork can go.

  a) THE MACHINE. Most digital envelope presses cannot print within about 5 mm of
     any edge, and cannot print over the gummed flap or the double-thickness seams
     down the sides. The Klyara return address sits 12 mm in from the left and
     11 mm down from the top, which clears all of that on both sizes.

  b) THE WINDOW. Two window positions are common in the UK and suppliers differ.
     Both are drawn on the -guide files:

       DL, 220 x 110 mm
         Window A   90 x 45 mm, 20 mm from the left edge, 15 mm up from the foot
         Window B   89 x 39 mm, 20 mm from the left edge, 18 mm up from the foot

       C5, 229 x 162 mm
         Window A   100 x 45 mm, 20 mm from the left edge, 55 mm up from the foot
         Window B   90 x 45 mm, 25 mm from the left edge, 57 mm up from the foot

     Check the actual window against the envelope you are buying before you order
     a print run. The artwork clears both variants on both sizes, so if your
     supplier's window is anywhere in that region you are covered.

  c) ROYAL MAIL. Two areas must stay clear of any printing:

       The bottom 15 mm of the whole face. This is where the sorting machines
       spray the barcode. Nothing may be printed there, in any colour.

       The top right corner, roughly 40 x 40 mm, for the stamp or the frank.

     Both are drawn on the -guide files in dashed red.

  d) RETURN ADDRESS. It is on the front, top left, which is the usual place for a
     small firm and keeps it well clear of the window. If you would rather follow
     Royal Mail's own preference, move it to the back flap: it is a single block
     of text and moving it is a two-line change in the generator.

  Send the printer the -artwork file. Send the -guide file as a reference only,
  and say plainly that the dashed lines are not to be printed.

5. IF SOMETHING HAS TO CHANGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every file here is generated by a script, so a change is a one-line edit and a
rebuild rather than a redraw:

  /root/klyara/v2/build/04_brand/kprint.py        bleed, crop marks, the mark,
                                                  the QR codes, the icon set
  /root/klyara/v2/build/04_brand/cards.py         business cards, names and roles
  /root/klyara/v2/build/04_brand/stationery.py    letterhead, slip, envelopes
  /root/klyara/v2/build/04_brand/letterhead_docx.py  the editable Word letterhead
  /root/klyara/v2/build/04_brand/sheets.py        service sheets, plan content
  /root/klyara/v2/build/04_brand/leaflet.py       the A5 leaflet
  /root/klyara/v2/build/04_brand/onepager.py      the brand sheet
  /root/klyara/v2/build/04_brand/signature.py     the email signatures
  /root/klyara/v2/build/04_brand/assets.py        the PNG logo files Word needs

The names, roles and email addresses on the business cards are a dictionary at
the top of cards.py. The plan prices and everything listed under each plan are a
list at the top of sheets.py. Nothing is typed twice.

6. ONE THING THAT IS DELIBERATELY MISSING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

There is no professional body logo, no licence number and no "AML supervised by"
line anywhere in this pack, because the supervision is not in place yet. The
email signature carries that line already written and commented out. Unhide it
the day the practice licence arrives, add it to the letterhead footer at the same
time, and not before.
