Why an app
and not just a website?

Because the paperwork moment happens at the trade counter, not at a desk. The app exists for the ten seconds when the receipt is in your hand and the engine is running: camera, snap, gone. Everything else it does is a bonus built on that habit.

  • Approve payroll and see payslips from anywhere
  • Every call, chat and chase the robots handled, logged

Setup takes
four minutes.

Step 1

Get your invite

Your login is issued during onboarding: an email with your link and a one-time code. No public signup, because your books are not public.

Step 2

Install and sign in

App Store or Google Play, sign in with the invite, add face/fingerprint unlock. Two-factor is on by default: it is your financial data.

Step 3

Snap your first receipt

We demo it live on your phone during onboarding with a real crumpled receipt. Ten seconds later it is in the books, coded, filed.

Step 4

Forget about it

The correct amount of admin to think about is none. Nudges arrive when you are needed; everything else just happens.
