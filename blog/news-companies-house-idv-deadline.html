Companies House identity checks: the director deadline is real

8 Jul 2026 · 9 min read · by the Klyara team

30 second versionBreak it down for me

  • Identity verification is now compulsory for new directors and PSCs, and rolls through existing ones at confirmation-statement time.
  • Verification takes about ten minutes online with photo ID, or via an authorised agent.
  • Unverified directors will be unable to file, and acting while unverified is an offence.
  • Every director and person with significant control needs this, including spouses holding shares.

The Companies House reforms have reached the part that touches every limited company builder personally. Identity verification, introduced by the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act, is now being enforced through the filing system: new directors verify at incorporation, and existing directors are being swept in as their confirmation statements fall due.

What you actually have to do

  1. 1
    Check who needs it

    Every director and every person with significant control. In a family company that routinely includes a spouse holding shares.

  2. 2
    Verify online

    GOV.UK One Login with photo ID: passport or driving licence and a face scan. Ten to fifteen minutes, free.

  3. 3
    Or verify through an agent

    Authorised agents (including accountants registered for the purpose) can verify you instead.

  4. 4
    Note your personal code

    Verification produces a code that links your identity to your Companies House records. It gets used at filing time.

  5. 5
    Do it before the confirmation statement

    Waiting until a filing is blocked turns ten minutes of admin into a company that cannot file.

The consequence of ignoring it

A company whose officers are unverified will find filings rejected, and continuing to act as an unverified director is itself an offence with fines attached. This is not a soft launch: rejections are already happening.

What happens if you do nothing

StageConsequence
Confirmation statement falls dueFiling is blocked without verified identities
Filings become overdueLate filing penalties begin accruing
Continued trading unverifiedActing as an unverified director is an offence
Register shows overdue filingsVisible to customers, suppliers and credit agencies

The last row is the commercial one: your Companies House record is public, and commercial clients do check it.

It affects more people than directors

Persons with significant control must verify too, which in a typical family company means a spouse holding shares who has never dealt with Companies House in their life. Check who is on your PSC register before assuming this is a one-person job.

Doing it now, properly

  1. 1
    Gather photo ID

    Passport or driving licence, plus a phone with a working camera.

  2. 2
    Use GOV.UK One Login

    The same identity system used across government services. Free.

  3. 3
    Complete the face check

    A short liveness scan matching you to the document.

  4. 4
    Record your identity code

    It links your verified identity to Companies House filings.

  5. 5
    Repeat for every director and PSC

    One verified director does not cover the company.

Ten minutes each, once, permanently. Compared with a blocked confirmation statement and a public record showing overdue filings, it is the cheapest compliance task on the calendar.
