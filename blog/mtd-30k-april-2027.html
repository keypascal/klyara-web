Making Tax Digital, the 30k wave

Earning 30k or more? You are in Making Tax Digital from April 2027

3 August 2026, 10 min read · by the Klyara team

30 second versionBreak it down for me

  • From April 2027 the MTD threshold drops to 30,000 pounds of qualifying income.
  • Gross counts: invoice 35k and bank 28k after CIS and you are still in scope.
  • Self-employment and property income stack together, per person.
  • The 2026-27 tax year is your practice run. Use it and day one is a non-event.

The first wave of Making Tax Digital for Income Tax, the over-50k crowd, went live in April 2026, and predictably half of them found out from a brown envelope. You have the chance to do this the easy way, because your wave has a date: 6 April 2027, when the threshold drops to 30,000 pounds.

Let us be blunt about who that catches: pretty much every full-time subcontractor in the country. Thirty thousand gross is 600 a week invoiced. A 160 day rate, four days a week, gets you there with three weeks off. If you work full-time on the tools, you are in this wave.

Thirty thousand of what, exactly?

Qualifying income is your gross self-employment income plus any property income, measured before expenses and, the bit that stings, before CIS deductions. The 20 percent your contractors hold back still counts as your income. It is your money; HMRC just met it before you did.

Rule of thumb: if the totals on your invoices for the year add to more than 30,000, plan on being in from April 2027. What lands in the bank is irrelevant to the test.

What actually changes for you

  • Digital records become mandatory. Every invoice and expense captured in HMRC-recognised software as you go. The big bag of receipts is formally retired.
  • Four quarterly updates a year: summaries of income and expenses, filed from that software shortly after each quarter ends.
  • A final declaration wraps the year. That is where tax is calculated and settled, fed by a year of live data.

Miss deadlines and the points-based penalty system starts collecting. One slip is survivable; a habit gets expensive.

Why the 2026-27 year is your secret weapon

Your first MTD quarter will draw on habits you have either built or have not. Start keeping clean digital records a year early and April 2027 becomes a non-event: records already digital, categories already right, CIS deductions already tracked. The first quarterly update takes minutes because the year behind it was tidy.

Start in March 2027 instead and you will be learning software, reconstructing a year of receipts and hitting your first HMRC deadline simultaneously. Same regime, ten times the pain.

Your twelve-month run-up, in four moves

  1. Confirm your wave. Two taps on the MTD checker. No sign-up, straight answer.
  2. Go digital now, gently. Snap receipts into an app the day you get them. Ten seconds each, and the year builds itself.
  3. Get the CIS ducks in a row. Deduction statements filed, gross or net status confirmed, materials invoiced separately where they should be.
  4. Decide who files. DIY with software is possible if you enjoy this sort of thing. If you would rather never think about it, that is what Klyara Solo is for: 59 a month, everything handled, and joining the waitlist locks the founding rate through the 2027 rush.

The honest upside

Quarterly figures mean you finally see what you earn while you can still do something about it: raise a day rate, drop a slow payer, buy the van before year end instead of after. And your CIS refund stops being an annual guess. MTD is a compliance headache that accidentally makes you better informed than you have ever been. We would never have designed it this way, but we will take the silver lining.

In the 30k wave? Join the Solo waitlist: 59 pound founding rate locked, set up well before April 2027, first quarterly update filed while everyone else is still googling what MTD stands for.

Working out your own position

  1. 1
    Open your last tax return

    You want the turnover box, not profit, and any property income figures.

  2. 2
    Add them together

    Qualifying income stacks self-employment and property, per person, per tax year.

  3. 3
    Use the gross figure

    What you invoiced before CIS deductions, not what reached the bank.

  4. 4
    Compare against 30,000

    Over it, your date is April 2027. Well over 50,000 and you are already in.

  5. 5
    Diarise the prep

    Software this month, receipt habit this quarter, practice quarter before it counts.

Why the practice year matters

Firms that started voluntarily in 2026 describe their first mandatory quarter as unremarkable. Firms that started in the week it became compulsory describe it very differently. The system is not hard; learning it under a deadline is.

What to do with the months in between

The single highest-value change is not software, it is a separate business bank account with every business pound flowing through it. That one decision turns your bank feed into most of your bookkeeping and removes the coffee-by-coffee sorting that makes mixed accounts miserable. Add the receipt habit, let software assemble the quarters, and the compliance question answers itself.
