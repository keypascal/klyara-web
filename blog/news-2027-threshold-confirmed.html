Confirmed: the 30,000 pound MTD wave lands April 2027

10 Jun 2026 · 9 min read · by the Klyara team

30 second versionBreak it down for me

  • HMRC has confirmed the qualifying income threshold drops to 30,000 pounds from April 2027.
  • The measure is gross qualifying income from your 2025-26 return: turnover plus property income, before CIS deductions.
  • A subbie invoicing 35,000 and banking 28,000 after deductions is in scope.
  • The preparation year is now: software, the receipt habit and a practice quarter before it counts.

The second wave of Making Tax Digital now has a confirmed date. From April 2027, sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over 30,000 pounds join the quarterly system, and for the construction trade this is the big one: the threshold sits below a typical full-time subcontractor's invoiced year.

Who this catches

The measureGrossinvoiced turnover plus property income, per person
Not the measureBankedCIS deductions do not reduce it
The test year2025-26your return for that year decides your 2027 status

The detail that moves thousands of subbies a year earlier than they expect: HMRC counts what you invoiced, not what reached the bank. Invoice 35,000 pounds of labour and bank 28,000 after the 20 percent comes off, and the figure HMRC tests is 35,000. Add any rental income on top, because qualifying income stacks self-employment and property together, per person.

Do the check tonight

Open your last tax return. Turnover box, not profit, before deductions, plus any property income. Over 30,000: your date is April 2027 and your preparation year has already started. Over 50,000: you should already be filing quarterly.

Using the year well

  1. 1
    This month

    Separate business bank account and recognised software, bank feed on.

  2. 2
    This quarter

    The receipt habit: photograph everything at purchase, ten seconds, no exceptions.

  3. 3
    This year

    Run a voluntary practice quarter so the first real one is your second, not your first.

  4. 4
    Before April 2027

    Agent authorisation if someone files for you: the postal step takes a week you cannot compress in deadline season.

The full picture, thresholds to penalties, is in the free guide. The MTD guide for tradesmen.

What the confirmation actually changes

The threshold itself was already published; what has firmed up is that it is now embedded in the regulations and timetable rather than sitting in consultation. For planning purposes that matters: firms can now safely invest a year in preparation knowing the date will not move again.

  • Now to April 2027
    The preparation year

    Software, separate account, receipt habit, optionally a voluntary practice quarter.

  • 6 April 2027
    Mandation

    Digital records become compulsory for the 30k-plus group from the first transaction of the year.

  • 7 August 2027
    First update due

    The opening quarter of the new regime, 6 April to 5 July.

  • 31 January 2029
    First final declaration under MTD

    Settling the 2027-28 year. Payment dates unchanged.

A year of preparation, then a rhythm. The firms that use the year describe day one as uneventful.

A quiet detail worth knowing

Voluntary sign-up is open now. Joining early means your first mandatory quarter is your fourth or fifth actual one, and mistakes made voluntarily carry no penalties. Nobody advertises this, and it is the cheapest insurance available.

Your 2026-27 preparation list

  • Business bank account separated from personal, if not already
  • Recognised software chosen and the bank feed switched on
  • CIS switch enabled in software so income records gross
  • Receipts photographed at purchase, every time, starting today
  • One voluntary quarter filed as a dry run before it counts
