Regulation radar

What is hitting
construction in 2026.

The rule changes rolling through this year and next, in date order, in plain English, with what to actually do about each one. Bookmark it; we keep it current.

April 2026, in force now

MTD for Income Tax goes live at £50,000

Sole traders and landlords over £50k qualifying income must keep digital records and file quarterly updates from recognised software, with a final declaration at year end. Gross income counts, before CIS deductions. Penalty points are already accruing for late filers.

Do this: over £50k and not filing quarterly? Sort it this month. Klyara Solo takes the whole job for £59 a month.

Rolling through 2026

Companies House identity verification, every director

Under the new Companies House rules, new directors have needed verified identity since November 2025, and existing directors must verify by their next confirmation statement, with deadlines rolling through autumn 2026. It is free, takes minutes, and ignoring it blocks your filings.

Do this: every director verifies now rather than at the deadline. We nudge our Ltd clients ahead of their confirmation-statement date.

April 2027

MTD threshold drops to £30,000

The big one for the trade: most full-time subbies clear £30k gross without trying. Digital records and four quarterly updates a year become mandatory for the bulk of self-employed construction.

Do this: spend the 2026-27 tax year getting records digital so day one is a non-event. The Solo waitlist locks a £59 founding rate.

April 2028

MTD threshold drops again, to £20,000

By this point it is essentially every self-employed tradesperson bar part-timers. The direction of travel is one way; the only choice left is whether you join calmly or in a panic.

Do this: nothing urgent if you are only in this wave, but snapping receipts into an app from now costs ten seconds a day and removes the cliff entirely.

The standing rules
that never sleep.

Not new, still catching firms out every single month. The permanent fixtures on the compliance calendar.

CIS returns, the 19th, monthly

Contractors file every month, even a nil return. Subbies verified before first payment, statements issued after. Penalties start at £100 per late return and climb.

VAT domestic reverse charge

In force since March 2021 and still wrecking invoices. Most B2B construction work between VAT and CIS registered firms: no VAT on the invoice, customer accounts for it. The plain-English guide.

Payroll RTI, on or before payday

Every pay run reports to HMRC in real time, plus pension auto-enrolment duties for the PAYE crew. Weekly-paid crews mean fifty-two deadlines a year, which is why ours run on rails.

MTD penalty points

The points-based regime behind every MTD deadline: each late submission is a point, points become fixed fines, interest rides on late payment. Zero points is a habit, not luck.

Dates correct at August 2026. Rules move; before acting, check gov.uk or ask us. The check is free.
